SELANGOR - RESIDENTIAL

The state Selangor in Malaysia, saw 27,673 residential property transactions from August 2025 to September 2026 from 3,777 projects/townships. The median psf was RM361, while the median property price stood at RM450,000. Half of the transactions occurred between RM300,000 and RM709,795, based on the 25th to 75th percentile.
