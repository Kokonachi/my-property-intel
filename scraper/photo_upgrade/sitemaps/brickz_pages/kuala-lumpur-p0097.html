KUALA LUMPUR - RESIDENTIAL

Located in Malaysia, the state Kuala Lumpur recorded 11,925 residential property transactions from August 2025 to September 2026 from 1,567 projects/townships. The median price per square foot was RM574, with a median property price of RM694,000. Transaction values ranged from RM400,000 to RM1,350,000, as per the 25th to 75th percentile.
