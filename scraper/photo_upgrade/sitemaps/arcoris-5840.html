Discover Arcoris Residences

Arcoris Residences

Arcoris Residences is a beautifully designed and unique development located in Mont Kiara, Kuala Lumpur. The construction of this development was done by the famous UEM Sunrise, a very well reputed company in the construction field. The development of this leasehold project was completed in the year 2016 and it contains all the facilities, and features to make it a top development of the city.

Concept

Arcoris Residences contains a lot of facilities and features that the residents of many new developments can only dream of having. There is a hotel and plaza in the development. There is a multi-purpose hall which can be used for different occasions by the residents. Residentst can also enjoy sauna facility as well. There is a lovely barbecue area in the development where the residents can spend some unforgettable time with their family and friends.

Accessibility and Amenities near Arcoris Residences

Arcoris Residences was developed strategically, keeping in mind the importance of accessibility of the location. The residents do not have to worry about moving to and from the location. Public transportation is also available in the locality, making the area of the development even more attractive. The residents of the development can use the Kepong Sentral KTM Station, Kepong KTM Station, Kepong KTM Station, Segambut KTM Station and Putra KTM Station for commutes.



There is an upcoming MRT Station in that area but the nearest station currently is MRT Semantan, which is approximately a 13 minutes’ drive. The development is easily accessible and is connected via major roads and highways of the area, from where the residents can reach any place in the city easily just by driving.

Facilities and Project Details

Arcoris Residences is a beautiful development consisting of 2 blocks. There are 262 business suites, 275 hotel room units, 336 SoHo units and 331 serviced units in the development. The buyers have the option to select from different designs of the units. The built-up area of the serviced units in the development starts from 850 sq ft.



The development offers some other facilities and features as well such as a wading pool, a tennis court, pool car, lap pool, badminton courts and a great gymnasium equipped with modern exercising machines where the residents can work out while remaining in the development and enjoy a healthy lifestyle. The development also offers complete car parking facility for its residents. Most importantly, the development is equipped with latest security gadgets and provides 24-hour security to its residents to make sure that the residents are protected from any kind of external threats.

Schools/Colleges/Education Institute

There are many famous educational institutions in the area, providing top class education services to the people of the locality. Few of the famous educational institutions near the development are namely Garden International School Kuala Lumpur, SMK Seri Hartamas, SMK Segambut Jaya, SMK Taman Tun Dr Ismail and Bukit Damansara Primary School.

Medical Centres

There are many famous medical clinics and hospitals in the area where the residents can go in case of any medical situation. Few of the famous medical clinics and hospitals near the development are namely Global Doctors Hospital, HE Medical Clinic, and Kiara Medical Centre.

Shops/Shopping Malls

It is considered to be a great place for those who love to shop because there are many famous shopping spots in the area. Few of the famous shopping centers near the locality are namely 1 Mont Kiara, 163 Retail Park, Hartamas Shopping Centre and Plaza Mont Kiara from where the residents can shop for almost anything they want.
